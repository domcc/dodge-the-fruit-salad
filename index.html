Dodge The Fruit Salad

You're a banana kid! Run to the portal before time runs out, then fly the fruit bowl to the next level. Can you beat all 80 levels?

  • Jump: Space / ↑ / W  ·  Duck: ↓ / S  ·  Run faster: → / D  ·  Sound: M  ·  Music: N  ·  Pause: P
  • Blueberries and knives get you straight away. Raspberries, watermelons and rolling rock melons drain your energy. Banana peels make you slip!
  • Splash through puddles and grab sunshine for energy. Every 5th one gives a big energy boost, or a ❤️ if you're full. A ❤️ saves you once!
  • Jump on the cutting boards to go higher. Every 20th level a fruit alien chases you. Don't trip! Every 30th level is a volcano obstacle course.
  • On a phone: tap the right side to jump, hold the left side to duck

Best level: 1
